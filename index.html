Important:

For children and teenagers, BMI is interpreted according to age and sex using BMI-for-age percentile growth charts.

The standard categories are:

• Underweight: Below 5th percentile
• Healthy weight: 5th to below 85th percentile
• Overweight: 85th to below 95th percentile
• Obesity: 95th percentile or above

A healthcare professional should interpret the child's percentile and growth pattern.
